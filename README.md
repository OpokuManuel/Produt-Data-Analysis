# Product Sales & Revenue Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end data analysis project exploring transaction trends, revenue distribution, customer segments, and product performance across 2,000 retail sales records.

---

## Executive Summary & Key Metrics

* **Total Revenue Generated:** $2,452,084.30
* **Total Transactions:** 2,000 Orders
* **Total Units Sold:** 5,552 Units
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** $1,226.04

---

## Key Insights & Findings

### 1. Product Category Dominance
* **Computers** is the primary driver of top-line growth, generating **$1.35M (54.9%)** of total revenue.
* **Monitors** generated **$526.7K**, followed by **Networking ($259.5K)** and **Storage ($229.8K)**.

### 2. Top 5 Revenue-Generating Products
1. **Gaming Laptop:** $432,576.06
2. **Laptop Air 13:** $273,532.50
3. **Laptop Pro 14:** $263,486.40
4. **All-in-One PC:** $254,994.00
5. **Ultrawide Monitor:** $190,959.45

### 3. Regional & Channel Performance
* **Top Region:** The **North Region** led in total sales with **$561.8K**, closely followed by the **Central Region ($556.7K)**.
* **Primary Channel:** **Online sales** accounted for the largest volume at **$791.5K**, followed by **Corporate Sales ($718.7K)** and **Retail Stores ($631.4K)**.

### 4. Customer Segmentation
* **Returning Customers** accounted for **68.6% ($1.68M)** of total sales, indicating high brand retention and repeat purchase value compared to **New Customers ($768.8K)**.
